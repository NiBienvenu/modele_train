Imyaka icumi mu gacerere : ukuburwa irengero kwa Yohani Bigirimana ni ikimenyetso c'ukudahana ivyaha ku mabi akorerwa abamenyeshamakuru mu Burundi
Umumenyeshamakuru w'ikinyamakuru Iwacu Yohani Bigirimana yarabuzwe irengero kw'igenekerezo rya 22 Mukakaro mu mwaka w'2016, vyikekwako yoba yafashwe n'abo mw'iperereza, gushika ubu ntiyigeze atorwa. Inyuma y'imyaka icumi, amatohoza nta nyishu yatanze yoshira ahabona ukuri. Ishirahamwe RSF, ry'Abamenyeshamakuru batagira imbibe ririyamiriza ukwo kudahana ivyaha, rigatera akamo ubutegetsi ribusaba kuva mu gacerere hagatangwa umuco kuvy'uwo mumenyeshamakuru.
« Inyishu yama itangwa kuwibaraguza abaza ivy'uwo mumenyeshamakuru yabuzwe irengero irazwi : ngo ''amatohoza arabandanya''. Ariko ni uruhendo kuba ari ukumwikura. Twarabajije ico kibazo incuro nyinshi, inyishu yama ari iyo ». Uwuyobozi w'uruganda rwo kumenyesha amakuru rw'ikinyamakuru Iwacu, Abbas Mbazumutima abibona co kimwe n'Ishirahamwe RSF, ry'Abamenyeshamakuru batagira imbibe : uno musi nta mutegetsi n'umwe ashaka gutanga umuco ku bijanye n'umumenyeshamakuru Yohani Bigirimana yabuzwe irengero.
Umutambukanyi wiwe, Godeberthe Hakizimana aridoga avugako yitwaye mu giporisi c'inyamiramabi mu kwezi kw'umunani mu mwaka wa 2016 ariko atacavuyemwo. Ati ''sinzi ko iyo dosiye itarubikwa canke ko amatohoza akibandanya''. Arongera ati ''nta mutegetsi arigera anyakura ku bijanye n'ivyo''.
Kw'igenekerezo rya 22 Mukakaro umwaka wa 2016, Yohani Bigirimana, umumenyeshamakuru w'ikinyamakuru cigenga Iwacu gikorera mu Burundi, yari afise imyaka 37, ''yazindutse aja gukora, ariko ntiyigeze atahuka'', nk'uko uwo mupfakazi abivuga.
Jean Bigirimana yari yitavye umuntu amwemereye amakuru i Bugarama, ni ku birometero 40 mu buraruko bw'igisagara ca Bujumbura, aho yafatwa n'abantu bikekwa ko bakorera iperereza baca bamwuriza ku nguvu mu muduga ufise vya biyo udahuta ubona abari muriyo, nk'uko vyamenyeshejwe n'ababibonye.
« N'aho atari bwanyuruzwe, Yohani Bigirimana yaguma aterwa ubwoba n'abantu bamubwira ko akorana n'abatavuga rumwe nareta. » Ivyo bimenyeshwa n'umutambukanyi wiwe Godeberthe Hakizimana “Avant l'enlèvement, il recevait des menaces. On lui disait qu'il était du côté des opposants”
Inyuma y'aho, mu misi mike haratowe mu ruzi Mubarazi ibiziga bibiri bimaze kwononekara. Uwo mupfasoni yaratumweko kugirango yihweze ko atomenya ko hoba harimwo umunega wiwe.
Yasanze ari wenyene ata kiremesha, imbere y'ikiziga giciwe umutwe, ahagatiwe n'abaporisi. Atekewe n'ubwoba n'agahinda ntiyashoboye kwemeza ko yoba ari umugabo wiwe. Nya biziga uko ari bibiri vyaciye bijanwa guhambwa ata matohoza nk'ayitwa ADN abikozweko.
Inyuma y'imyaka icumi irangiye Yohani Bigirimana atawuzi irengero ryiwe, ubutegetsi bukaguma mu gacerere, ni ikimenyetso c'ukudahana ivyaha kudashobora kubandanya. Inyuma y'imyaka icumi, umutambukanyi wiwe, incuti, n'umuryango w'abamenyeshamakuru bararindiriye, n'aho amaso yaheze mu nzira, ko ukuri kwomenyekana. Nta n'uwuzi ko amatohoza akibandanya.
« Mu gihe abategetsi bo mu Burundi bemeza ko bashize imbere ubutungane, bari bakwiye kuvyerekana mu gutanga umuco kubijanye n'umumenyeshamakuru Yohani Bigirimana, bakamenyesha icese aho amatohoza ageze, gurtyo bakanahagarika ukudahana ivyaha kur'uko kubura irengero ry'uwo mumenyeshamakuru kwaranze umwuga wo kumenyesha amakuru mur'ino myaka ».
Ivyo bimenyeshwa na Jeanne Lagarde, Umuyobozi w'igisata kijejwe ugutabariza mw'ishirahamwe RSF ry'abamenyeshamakuru batagira imbibe ku mugabane wa Afrika iri mu bumanuko bwa Sahara.
Ikinyamakuru Iwacu kimaze kwitwara mu kwezi kw'umunani mu mwaka wa 2016, ubutegetsi bwaramenyesheje ko hagiye gutangura amatohoza, mbere agakorwa na Parake y'i Muramvya. Ariko yaba abamenyeshamakuru bakorana na Yohani Bigirimana, baba ababonnye uwo mumenyeshamakuru afatwa, ntawigeze yumvirizwa n'igiporisi.
Kugirango haboneke inyishu kuvyo amatohoza yoba yaragezeko, Ishirahamwe RSF ryaragerageje kenshi kuvugana n'Umushingwamanza kizigenza wa Repuburika, Rose Nkorerimana, n'umukuru wa Sentare ntahinyuzwa, Gamaliel Nkurunziza, n'umuvugizi w'iyo Sentare, Anyesi Bangiricenge ariko ntacavuyemwo.
Mu kwezi kw'indwi kw'umwaka wa 2023, Umukuru w'igihugu c'Uburundi, Varisito Ndayishimiye yaramenyesheje ko atawobo mu gihugu kitagira ubutungane.
“Yohani yangendera ku mategeko''
Arangije ivyigwa bijanye n'amategeko, Yohani Bigirimana, yinjiye mu mwuga w'ukumenyesha amakuru mu 2010 aho yahereye muri radiyo Rema FM. Iyo radiyo isambuwe mu gihe intwaro y'umukuru w'igihugu Petero Nkurunziza yahakwa gutembegazwa, uwo mumenyeshamakuru na bagenziwe baciye baja mu kinyamakuru Iwacu mu gisata c'iradiyo ivugira ku rubuga ngurukanabumenyi.
« Kwari ukugirango, have agacere karangwa inyuma y'aho amaradio atari make yigenga asamburiwe », nk'uko bivugwa n'umumenyeshamakuru yakoranye na Yohani Bigirimana atashimye ko izina ryiwe rivugwa.
Nk'umunyamategeko, Yohani Bigirimana yakora cane cane ibiganiro bijanye n'ivy'imanza. Ariko yarakunda n'ibijanye n'ibidikikije, ihindagurika ry'ibihe n'ibindi.
Nk'uko uwo mumenyeshamakuru yakoranye na Yohani Bigirimana abandanya abimenyesha ngo hari ivyabaye imbere n'unyuma y'aho aburiwe irengero. Ngo ''harabaye ukwinengesera bidasigura guhesha, ahubwo ari ukubandanya umwuga hisunzwe amategeko awugenga ata gutamikwa ijambo, nyabuna ibinyamakuru bikavugira abimwe ijambo, babuze aho boriserurira, mu gukora inkuru nyazo, zimwe hari abakorako bagasha''.
Mu bisabwa n'umutambukanyi wa Yohani Bigirimana, ngo aripfuza ko ''ivyashikiye uwo mumenyeshamakuru bitokwibagirana''. Ngo ''birakenewe ko ukuri kuja ahabona, ubutungane bugakora, ivy'uwo mumenyeshamakuru bikabandanya bivugwa''.
Ikinyamakuru Iwacu nticoroherezwa
Ico kinyamakuru kizwi mukudahengeshanya kwisunga amategeko n'akarangamutima bigenga umwuga cama caturatujwe.
Mu mwaka wa 2019, abamenyeshamakuru baco 4 bakorera barafatiwe i Musigati mu buraruko bushira uburengero bw'Uburundi, bari bagiye gutohoza ku nkuru ijanye n'umugwi ugwanya intwaro ya Petero Nkurunziza wari wateye mur'ako karere uzananye mu buseruko bwa Repuburika iharanira demokarasi ya Kongo. Abo bamenyeshamakuru bamaze imisi 430 bapfunzwe.
Si ivyo gusa urubuga ngurukana bumenyi bw'ikinyamakuru Iwacu rwarugawe mu Burundi kuva mu 2017 gushika mu 2022. Ishirahamwe RSF ry'abamenyeshamakuru batagira imbibe, ribicishije ku buhinga bwaryo bwiswe Collateral Freedom, ryarashizeho izindi mbuga z'ikinyamakuru Iwacu, aho abasomyi bari hagati mu gihugu bashobora guca mu gusoma amakuru ya Iwacu.
