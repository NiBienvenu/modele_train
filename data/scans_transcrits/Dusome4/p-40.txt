- Emwe urarinda kandi uri ubwenge. None ko nari n'ijambo nje kukwibariza worindonsa ?
- Ni amaki ga Gakwa ?
- Ntabara mugenzi unyongerereze ubwenge ndaburushe ibindi bikoko vyose biba muri iri shamba.
- Banza ugende unzanire umukuza wuzuye inyoni, icansi cuzuye amata y'imbogo, hamwe n'imvuvyi ireha n'iri cumu nitwaje. Ukaramuka ubinzaniye nzoheza ndakwongerereze ubwenge.
Gakwavu gahitana bimwe wa muntu yakabariye, kamanuka ku mwonga, aho ibikoko vyose biza kunywa. Umurango uramye, inyoni zitororokanye, zegera aho zinywa amazi zizwiragira, zitambatamba mu mashami y'ibiti. Gakwavu gasokoroka aho kari kikinze, gahitana wa mukuza kegera umugezi, gatangura kuziyegereza ku rwenge kivugana gati :
- Eka da ! Yarambeshe wa murinzi w'ishamba. Ngo inyoni zokwuzura uyu mukuza. Zobigenza gute ? Za nyoni ziba zakumvise nk'ejo zigahundarako. Zikabaza ziti :
- Ugize ngw'iki ga Gakwa ? Gati :
- Ni uwampenze ngo mwokwuzura uyu mukuza. None mbona bitoshoboka. Za nyoni ziti :
- Ariko Gakwavu uri impari. Reka tukwereke.
Iya mbere irinjira iyindi irakurikira, zose zica zirangiriramwo, umukuza uranihira, kararumya, karaterura gatereka hejuru gatoyi karinumira.
Muri ako kanya imbogo irashika. Kayibonye gatangura kwivugana nk'umukecuru ngo :
- Ewe Gakwavu nkunda guhendwa. Ubwo imbogo yokwuzuza iki cansi amata ! Bishoboka bite ? Nanje ndaraba ! Imbogo irakebuka ikabaza iti :
- Utwo witonganya ni uduki ? Na ko gati :
- Ni uwampenze ngo wokwuzuza iki cansi amata, ariko nagende yarampenze. Imbogo iti :
Ha ! kibaye ikindi, na bitatu novyuzuza. Tega ico cansi wirabire. Gakwavu karatega. Ya mbogo irahagarara irivuruganya, ca cansi kiruzura. Gakwavu karitangaza, karajorerwa gati : [?]
- Ndashizwe, ibihari biraheze ! Gakwavu kagemanya ya mata kariraramira.
