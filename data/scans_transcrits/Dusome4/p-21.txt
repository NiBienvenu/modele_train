Icibagiwe co ni izonsa . Nta n'inkongoro y'umwana zikamwa . Aho yumviye iyo migambi y'ukugabanya inka, ishavu ryiriwe riramuniga. Umuhungu wiwe w'imfura Kagabo, atashe ku mugoroba, asanga se ariko aridoga, aturitsa urutoke, umwavyi na wo wamurenze . Ati :
- Mbega dawe ko ugorobeje unabiriwe vyagenze bite ? Se na we ati :
- Sigaho mwananje, hamwe mu gitondo, wapfuye gukura ikirenge ngaha, mbona abakuru bose ba komine, bari kumwe n'abandi ntazi bambaye imbone baratuye .
- None ngo barondera iki ga da ?
- Nabonye bagendura izo nka zanje, bongera baziharura, hanyuma bambarira ngo imigambi mishasha y'amajambere ishaka ko twozigabanya tugatunga nkeyi . None ngo nkenyere ndazirangirize ku macuniro zose, nsigarane nkeyi, ngo canke ngure izindi nshasha z'ikizungu !
Kagabo na we ati :
- Erega dawe ivyo bakubariye ni vyo. Uwo mugambi mushasha si mubi na gato !
Wofata arya mashashi mezameza ukayabangurira kuri rwa rupfizi rwa komine, izindi na zo ukazishora, mbona ari vyo vyoba vyiza .Kanatsinda, ngira ngo ni ho twosubira kunywa amata kuko numvise ngo izivyawe n'irya mpfizi zikamwa menshi .
Noneho zibaye nke, ni ho zorya zigahaga, mbona kirya kibara cacu kitagishobora n'ugukwira utwo tunyana .
Kandi ga da, dushoye izo nka zose, amafaranga turonse twoca tuyarimishamwo bwa bwatsi bushasha bama bahimiriza, kuko ngo burya ntibuvyibusha ni agatangaza .
Tubigenjeje gutyo, twoheza tugatunga inka nziza z'agahagarikashengero kandi zikamwa menshi.
Erega ubwo buka bw'umurengera ata co burya, nta co bumaze atari ukuba ingwizamurongo. Imigambi mishasha rero y'ukworora kijambere, ni ugukuraho vyinshi bibi, tugatunga bike vyiza.!!
