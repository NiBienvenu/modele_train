Vyakabaye kera, umugore abona ko bimugoye, agenda kubaza umupfumu, ati :
- Jewe umugabo wanje yambwiye ngo nze ndacumba, mpakure amarobe abiri, rimwe ry'Imana, irindi ryiwe, none vyananiye . Wa mupfumu ati :
- Ewe yaraguhenze : Imana ntirya, ni we yama abwirira . Kugira ngo umuhane, urashusha ibuye urishire aho wahora ushira bwa butsima, ikinyoma kizoja ahabona .
Wa mugore aratâha agira nk'uko umupfumu yamubwiye . Umugabo aje, aterura rya buye agira ngo ni irobe, arasha, aca ata hasi aromboka arasohoka . Haheze umwanya Bimari arinjira, araramutsa nka kumwe atacigeze . Umugore amuha amazi yo gukaraba . Bimari ngo ayakozemwo intoke, arashinyiriza . Umugore aramubaza ati :
- Mbega ntuze aho wabaye iki ? Bimari ati :
- Mu gitondo aho nariko ndashitura Maribori, nanyereye mu mase nkubita ibiganza mu gicaniro . Amapfa yarinze ahera, Bimari adasubiriye guhenda umugore .
