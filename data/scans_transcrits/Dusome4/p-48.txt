- Mwami w'i Burundi, ugucura umuntu ni igikorwa kigambaraye rwose . Turagusavye ko woturonderera ikigega cuzuye umunyota uvuye ku mishatsi y'abantu; n'ubwato bwuzuye amosozi .
Umwami rero akoranya abantu biwe bose iyo bava bakagera ngo nibabamwe, imishatsi bayiturire . Aba ari we abatangurira . Iminyota bashira mu kigega, haba nuko boronka uwuzimije intango yaco .
[image : abantu barira mu bwato, abandi bamwe imishatsi imbere y'ikigega]
Hanyuma baja mu vyo kurira . Babanza abagore n'abana ngo ni bo boyaronka ningoga na menshi, birabata . Umwami ngo nibabakubite barire. Barabakubita ariko biba ivy'ubusa . Umwami abonye biraniranye abwira Bugabobwigaba na se ngo nibigire, agirako arabagabira . Bugabobwigaba ngo abone yagire yicishe umutwe akahakurwa na se, aca asubira kw'izina ryiwe rya kera ari ryo Bimari .
IKINYOMA KIMARA UMUSI NTIKIMARA UMWAKA
Kera imbeba ntizaba mu bigega, zibera mw'ishamba zikanuranura nk'imende. Umve rero ingene zashitse mu bigega.
