- Narasizwe ! Arataha ntibugica. Erega bwakeye nyabarega yujuje. Kuva ubwo, nta handi bavumburira canke ngo bakuburire. Aca atandukira imiserege arimba, imbingobingo arateragira. Kuva ubwo, nyabarega zihaga umwavu n'ibivumburwa. Itongo ryiwe riboneraho riratabirwa, inkukura irarwanywa, riranaga, Futane n'abiwe bakira inzara.

UMUGABO YAGURANYE INKA UWUNDI
Habaye umugabo akitwa Nzomararumwe, agurana inka mugenzi we Ntakobatagize. Haciye igihe Ntakobatagize aje kwishuza, Nzomararumwe arinyegeza. asiga abwiye umugore wiwe ati :
- Aho azira hose umubwire ko nagiye kurondera ikinyamutitima c'umwami. Aca yikevya mu muyigwe w'amasaka aricara.
Ntibitevye. Ntakobatagize arashika, araramutsa, ataranamubaza n'iyo Nzomararumwe yagiye. wa mugore aritangaza ati :
- Emwe nuranyuranywe. yagiye kurondera ikinyamutitima c'umwami, kandi si uwuzogaruka vuba kuko ntikiva hafi. Ntakobatagize ati :
- Naje sinihuta nzomurindira. sinzogenda ntatahanye inka yanje. Aricara arinumira.
