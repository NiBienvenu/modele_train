Bukeye hageze icigwa c'ibiharuro, wa muhungu utanka, akora mu mufuko atamira intete . Uwo bicaranye amurya akara, Samandari amuha dukeyi . Emwe, na vyo ibiyoba bikaranze ntivyihisha . Uwuri inyuma aramoterwa, akwega ishati ya Samandari .
Umwigisha yari yumvise ako kamoto, akirondera iyo kava, ni ho yabona abo bahungu baryanye akara, aca arababaza ati :
- Ni amaki ? Uwuri inyuma ya Samandari ati :
- Motewe n'ibiyoba. Hoo! Samandari arafashwe! Aca aritanganiza ati :
- Jewe nari natekeye utuyoba, ngo bansabe ndabaha, kandi nanje nari nshonje . Abana bose batwengera rimwe . Umwigisha aca amubwira ati :
- Wazanywe no kurira mw'ishure no gusamaza abandi ? Samandari asaba ikigongwe ati :
- Singisubira .
***
Ntibitevye, Samandari bamutuma impuzu z'ishure . Ati :
- Ivyo vyo ntibingoye . Je Samandari n'aho batazozingurira, ko nzi guhiga inkwavu, nzohiga nka zine ndazishore . Baziraba bwa jisho . Ashitse i muhira abwira se ati :
- Badutumye impuzu nshasha . Kandi umukuru w'ishure yavuze ko atawuzosubirayo atazijanye . Se wiwe ati :
- Ico si co kizogukura mw'ishure . Nzopfuma nshora ya ntama, umwambaro wawe ntuzobura kuvamwo . Ejo uravyuka kare ugende uyinjanishije, isoko ireme twashitseyo .
Samandari si we yabonye burakeye ! Afata ya ntama ayiha ikiziriko, abwira se ko abaye aragenda . Se ati :
-Genda, yamara witegereze ntibayigutware . Samandari ati :
- Ingene ndayibabaye nyene, ntawuyintwara . Afata inzira y'isoko .
