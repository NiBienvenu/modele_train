Ugusozera
Irivuga ridahadikirije rishobora gukoreshwa mu buryo bwinshi. Hari aho rishobora kuba rukozi rw'igikorwa kivuzwe n'irindi ridahadikirije canke rikaba karangura. Iyo ryitangije integuza rica rifatwa nk'izina rigakora na none nka rukozi canke nka karangura.
Imyimenyerezo
Tora amavuga adahadikirije n'ayafashwe nk'izina muri aka gasomwa, mwongere muvuge ko ari rukozi canke karangura
Ukuvuga menshi si ko kuyamara. Nagiye kubibona atangura kwireshangishwa ata barinze kumubaza. Barukinamwo na we ntiyatanzwe kumudedaguza, aca atangura kwidodomba, arateba araduka kudondagura
