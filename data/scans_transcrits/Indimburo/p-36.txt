9. Sogokuru yavutse kuri Mwezi Gisabo.
10. Aho hambere bambara impuzu z'igiti.

12. Eee, erega sinamenye ko mwashitse !
Irivuga rihadikirijwe muri kahise ryerekana ko igikorwa cabaye kera canke cakozwe mu mwanya wamaze kurengana.
Iyo irivuga ryerekana ko haheze imyaka canke iminsi igikorwa kibaye, tuvuga ko rihadikirijwe muri kahise k'indengagihe.
Iyo irivuga ryerekana ko igikorwa cabaye mu mwanya w'uyu munsi tuvuga ko rihadikirijwe muri kahise k'impitakivi.
Kahise rero kari ubwoko bubiri : hariho kahise ka kera ari ko kahise k'indengagihe na kahise ka vuba ari ko kahise k'impitakivi.
a. Kahise k'indengagihe
Uturorero :
1. Kera narakubagana cane.
2. Imbwa n'akayabu vyankanye kuva kera.
3. Hirya y'ejo narumvise igitito ca Sankima.
4. Kera navuga ubureve none ubu mvuga neza.
5. Sogokuru yavutse kuri Mwezi Gisabo.
6. Aho hambere bambara impuzu z'inyagiti.
7. Kuva na kera na rindi abarundi barafashanya.
8. Mbega wararwaniye Uburundi kuri Rumariza ?
9. Ejo bundi twahuriye ku Ruvyironza.
Iyo irivuga rihadikirijwe muri kahise k'indengagihe tuvyerekwa n'uko mu ryungane hari mwo utujambo twerekana umwanya. Ni nk'utu : kera, aho hambere, kuva kera na rindi, ejo, hirya y'ejo, ku ndwi iheze, ku mwaka uheze, kare na kare, ejo bundi. Kahise k'indengagihe ushobora no kukumvira mw'ijwi ata kajambo kerekana umwanya kari mu ryungane canke ukavyerekwa n'igikorwa canke ikintu kiri mu ryungane cabaye kera.
Uturorero :
1. Sogokuru yavutse kuri Mwezi Gisabo
2. Mbega wararwaniye Uburundi kuri Rumariza ?
3. Warangiriye bimwe twavugana ?
4. Washitse ryari ? (Uvyumvira mw'ijwi ko ari kahise ka kera canke ka vuba).
b. Kahise k'impitakivi
Uturorero :
1. Yagusigarije amata makeyi wewe yanyoye icansi cose mu mwanya uheze
2. Yazindutse kare mu gatututuru ngira ngo ntiyibutse kuguhitana
3. Mwari kubonana avuye aha ubu nyene
4. Watevye nari kugusomya ku kagwa bangemuriye
5. Avuye aha ubu nyene
6. Eee, erega sinamenye ko mwashitse !
Iyo irivuga rihadikirijwe muri kahise k'impitakivi, hari aho tuvyerekwa n'utujambo : ubu nyene, kare mu gatondo, mu mwanya uheze... canke tukavyumvira mw'ijwi no mu ciyumviro kiri mu ryungane.
