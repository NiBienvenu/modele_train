I. Irivuga ridahadikirijwe
1.1. Muri aya mungane, nimwerekane amavuga yose adahadikirijwe n'ibice vyayo
- Abarundi basiba kurima ntibasiba kuvuga.
- Agiye kurongora igihugu imyaka itanu.
- Umwigeme wo hambere yategerezwa kumenya kurima, guteka, gucumba, guterera amata, gufata neza ivyo mu nzu, kujisha (ibirago, inkoko...), kwambara neza, gukubura, gusasa n'ibindi vyinshi.
- Amanutse kuraba mwenewabo mu mwonga.
- Ndikumana yagiye gukina abanje gusoma ca gitabu ciwe.
- Uriya muntu agiye kurima ari umwe.
- Yama ashaka gufasha bagenziwe iyo bari mu ngorane.
- Wa munyeshure yongeye kuronka amanota menshi mu kirundi.
- Yongeye guhura na sowanyu.
- Ni muze muriha ivyo mushobora kumara.
- Aja gushika igihe c'isaha cumi na zibiri zo ku mugoroba.
- Ni kuki uyu mwana adashaka guhora ? Nkeka ashaka kuryama.
1.2. Muri ayo mungane akurikira, erekana indobeko ziri mu mavuga adahadikirijwe
- Ashimye kumutwara mu muduga kuko ababaye cane.
- Bakwiye kurukubura abashitsi bataraza.
- Ntusubire kuhaturira kuko haca huma cane, uburyohe bugahera ; ahubwo ukwiye kuhareka hakavunda !
- Iyi nyana bahejeje kuyikura amahembe.
- Uribuka kubimubwira ataragenda.
- Burya bwato bahejeje kububaza ubu nyene.
- Ubwo ko ari akatsi gatoyi kamugiye mu jisho urashobora kukabona ? Ha ! N'aho wokabona nyene umengo vyokugora kukamukura kuko ususumira cane.
- Wa murima muzoja kuwusoroma ryari ?
- Rwa rugo rwari rwahenutse basubiriye kuruvyura.
- Mbe ni kuki urya muntu bakunda kumuvuga neza ?
- Nimubike neza izo mpuzu bazoza kuzitira ejo mu gitondo.
1.3. Nimuce akarongo munsi y'amavuga yagobotowe mu yandi
- Abandi bana ntibakunda kumuyagisha.
- Birabujijwe kurabira ku wundi mwana mu kibazo.
- Bwabo na Bwakira ntibari bagisiba gutukana muri iyo ndwi ishize ariko ubu barakundana.
- Ivyo urondera ko bidakunda kuboneka ?
- Si vyiza namba kuvugana indya mu kanwa.
- Nimube mwibuka kuruhuka iyo mwarushe.
- Ni vyiza guhanagura isuka uhejeje kurimisha.
- Ntiyamuhaye amafaranga ajana gusumisha.
- Muri irya minsi bari kurarira imirima yabo, bafashe ibisuma bine baca babijana kubipfungisha kw'i komine. Abaporisi bamaze kubikubitira imbere y'ishengero, baciye babona kubishira mw'ibohero.
- Uribuka kuntumira vya bintu wa mukozi.
- Urarondera isabuni yo kumesesha izo mpuzu.
- Yohana arakunda kuvomera imirima yiwe.
1.4. Mu rivuga rimwerimwe ryose, nimugobotoremwo amavuga atatatatu
Kuryama, gukora, kurima, kumena, kwiga, kurya, kwandika, gukira, kuvuga, kuraba, kumata, kubona, kwumva, kwicara, kumara, kuririmba, kwuma, kugenda, gusoma, kwubaka, kuvoma.
