2.2.2. Izihitishamwo
Ni nka :
- canke, cabure, kiretse, hako...
Uturorero
1. Uraza canke ureke.
2. Nzokuramutsa kiretse ntakubonye.
3. Musange yo cabure umurindirire aha.
2.2.3. Iz'amadidane canke z'impinyanyuro
Ni nka :
- yamara, ariko, mugabo, n'aho, kanatsinda, kumbure...
Uturorero :
1. Nari kwunguruzwa mugabo naciye ndwara
2. Ntiyigeze aza, kumbure vyamugoye.
3. Butoyi arazi ubwenge yamara ntanganya Buname.
4. Naho ingwe ikomeye, Bakame karayibasha.
5. Uyu munsi yazindutse iragwa ariko ntiyari nyinshi.
6. Arakabona arwaye kanatsinda ntiyitaho isuku ry'umubiri wiwe.
Izindi z'amadidane ni : niba, namba, hari naho, ngira ngo, ubibona, ubwo.
