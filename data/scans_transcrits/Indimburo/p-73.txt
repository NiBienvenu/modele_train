4.1.2. Nimuce akarongo munsi y'indongorazina ziri muri kino gisomwa
Abana b'imburakimazi
Umunsi umwe abana batatu barahitananye baja kw'ishure. Bageze mu nzira, umwe muri bo ati : "Nimuze uyu munsi dusibe". Uwundi na we ati : "None duca dusubira i muhira ?" Uwa gatatu na we ati : "Dusubiye i muhira abavyeyi bodutatira". Abo bana baca baja mw'ishamba kwaha inkere, bongera baja ku ruvuma. Isaha zitandatu zigeze abo bana bataha i muhira nk'uko vyahora kugira ngo abavyeyi babo ntibahinyure ko batagiye kw'ishure. Bahejeje gufungura, baca basubira muri rya shamba. Hageze ko abandi bataha na bo baca barataha. Bukeye, abigisha baca batuma ko abavyeyi babo. Gutyo baca baramenya ko abana babo bari basivye kw'ishure. Baca bababaza aho bari muri ico gihe. Abo bana baca barahanwa mu maso ya bagenzi babo.
4.2. Sahwanya
4.2.1. Nimuce akarongo munsi ya sahwanya ziri muri aya mungane mwongere muvuge ivyo zifatanya
- Umunebwe akunda kurya no kuryama.
- Imvura ya minsi yose irica imirima n'ibindi biterwa.
- Yari yavuze ko azoza ariko nkeka yagize ibimugora.
- Sinzi ko uno mwaka azomenya.
- Ntiyaje umunsi nari ndamwiteze kumbure azoza muri iyo ndwi iza.
- Arakabona atamenye kanatsinda ntiyiga bikwiye.
- Yoroye inka nyinshi kandi nziza.
- Muko ndabikwereka uzogira mbone.
- Amuhamagaye kugira ngo amutume.
- Nzoza kugusigurira ico cigwa kiretse mbuze umwanya.
- Akayabu n'imbeba birazirana urunuka.
- Nzoze ejo canke hirya y'ejo ?
- Yagiye ariko nta wuzi ko yashitse amahoro.
- Yari kuja kuramutsa inasenge ariko ntakigiye kuko baciye babonana.
- N'aho ari mukene aragerageza gufasha abo iwabo.
