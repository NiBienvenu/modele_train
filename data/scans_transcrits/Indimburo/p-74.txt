- Mbega wa mwana yarungurujwe ? Haba namba !
- Aho rero nzogaruka tubivugane neza.
- Eka data sindamukubita ijisho !
- Aba hakurya y'uriya musozi.
- Nta muntu yinjira ng'aha kuko twahashinguye ibintu bikomeye.
- Hirya y'ejo namye gutembera i Gihanga.
- Narabonanye na nyokwanyu, arakomeye buhorobuhoro.
- Mbega urazi neza ko Rozariya azoza mu rubanza ? Emwe, azoza !
b. Uzuza aya mungane n'intumbuzi zibereye
- Abana bariga kwandika no gusoma ... ikirundi.
- ... bushikana umusiba ku mugezi.
- Indero y'abana bo ... iratandukanye n'iy'urwaruka rw'...
- Womenya kwabaye ukwibagira canke yabigize ...
- Nta gakuru kiwe ? Ihibambewe duherukana...
4.4. Inkebuzo
Ca akarongo munsi y'inkebuzo ziri muri aya mungane
- Ewe mupfaso, ndindira ico ndakwibariza.
- Mbe ntu, mwomenya urupfunguzo rw'inzu narushize hehe ?
- Hewe, wamboneye bagumye ?
- Ewe mugenzi, urabanza ubitohoze neza !
- Yemwe abo ku muhana, muratwugamika ?
- Ewe mwana, kumbura uce mu jisho !
- Yemwe, ni muduhe utwo muri ko !
- Erega ngo nimucereze !
- Yereka ngo ntiwibagire ya mpuzu.
- Emwe bayo, umwana w'ingwe ni yo ngwe !
- Erega bakurondeye kuva kera !
- Eshe, haguruka tugende !
- Hewe, nyaruka turagiye!
- Erega yagutumyeko !
- Ewe sha, ingo ndakwitumire.
- Hemwe, nankana si ho muzoba muri ?
- Yemwe abo ng'aho, muraduha indaro ?
- Hemwe, iyo nkuru mwayumvise ?
- Mbega ntu, wasanze bimeze bite ?
4.5. Gatangazi
Tora gatangazi ziri muri aya mungane
- Yuu, wakabaye ubesha gutyo !
- Egwe ma, ko wari ufise abashitsi b'iteka wabura gushaza !
- Ashii, ndahembutse !
- Bacobo mwe, uru ruzuba tuja kurukira !
- Aho wa, wanzaniye bimwe nagutuma !
- Egwe da, weho uzokwunguruzwa !
- Ududuwe, ehe ingene bimunaniye gutambuka !
- Itu yaco ubesha nta nka nyinshi atunze.
4.6. Gakumbanya
a. Ca akarongo munsi ya gakumbanya ziri muri aya mungane
- Igihe sogokuru yatangura kuvuga ijambo twagiye twumva abantu bose ngo ce.
- Natereye urubuye mu ruzi nza numva ngo pwa.
- Ejo twagiye twumva umuturagaro ngo turi kandi ata mvura iriko iragwa.
- Mw'iradiyo babitse umuntu yitiranwa na senge nza numva umutima ngo vutu.
- Twageze mu Gihogazi imvura ari jejeje.
- Iyo bagandaye baguma bambaye impuzu zirabura tsikiri.
- Umuntu ari aho afise amenyo yera de.
