Amavuga yo mu mugwi wa kabiri atanga iciyumviro gikwiye. Ntakeneye kwisunga karangura kugira ngo aserure iciyumviro. Yonyene arikwiye.
Rabire aha :
1. Aravuga
2. Arakora
3. Aratekereza
4. Aracapa
5. Ndagenda
6. Ndacerewe
7. Ndagaruka
Ayo mavuga rero tuvuga ko ahadikirijwe mu mero nshimitsi. Iciyumviro atanga kirashimitse ; ni ukuvuga ko cikwiye. Ntakeneye kwisunga ayandi majambo ngo iciyumviro gikwire.
Umuntu avuze ati :
Buname aratekereza, nta kibazo gikenewe.
Ariko tuvuze duti :
Buname atekereza, umuntu yoca abaza ati :
Atekereza gute ? Canke nka nde ? ...
Amavuga ari muri nshimitsi tuyerekwa n'agace ra kari hagati muri yo.
1. Arakora / akora.
2. Aratekereza / atekereza.
3. Aravuga / avuga.
4. Aragaruka / agaruka.
Ayari muri nyoroshi na yo, nta gace ra afise.
Imyimenyerezo
Muri aya mungane, erekana amavuga ari mu mero nyoroshi n'ayari mu mero nshimitsi.
- Kabura aturukije inka.
- Ku mugoroba ndaza.
- Naho muvuga ndarwaye.
- Bimari ahamagaye inkuba.
- Kagabo araturukije.
- Umukenyezi wiwe amugaburira umutsima.
- Samandari arafashwe.
- Amazi akanye araryoshe.
- Kana aranezerwa n'ugusamaza abandi.
- Samandari akunda kurya cane ni co gituma avyibushe.
- Bakame karahenda ibindi bikoko.
- Mpunge urwaha yakimajije amaguru.
- Uwutazi akaraye araza ifu.
