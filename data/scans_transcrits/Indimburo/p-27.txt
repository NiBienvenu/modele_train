e. Insigarirazina-ngereka gaharura gatondeshamurongo
Uturorero :
1. Umunyeshure wa mirongo ine ku banyeshure mirongo ine nyene yunguruzwa ku gihamana.
2. Igice ca kabiri c'umwaka giheze, tuzosubira kubazwa.
3. Kw'isaha ya kane na ho duca tuja gukina.
4. Umwana wa mbere araja kuronka agashimwe.
Ayo majambo agomba kumera nk'amwe twabona ku nsigarirazina gaharura gatondeshamurongo. Na yo nyene araharura agashira ku rutonde mugabo ageretswe ku mazina kandi ikindi kiyatandukanya ni uko afise insigarirazina-ngereka nsanisha.
Itegereze neza
Utujambo "wa mbere" na "wa nyuma" ni insigarizina-ngereka gaharura gatondeshamurongo na two nyene.
Imyimenyerezo
Tora insigarirazina-ngereka gaharura gatondesha murongo woshira muri aya mungane.

f. Insigarirazina-ngereka kabaza
Uturorero :
1. Yashitse ku munsi uwuhe ?
2. Umaze imyaka ingahe uvutse ?
3. Ni ikihe giti cakubiswe n'inkuba ?
4. Ni abana bangahe bataje ?
5. Yagabanye inka zingahe ?
6. Ni uruhe rupapuro wanditse ko ?
7. Ni ibiti bingahe wiciye ?
8. Yaguhaye amafaranga angahe ?
Ayo majambo yose tubona aciwe munsi akarongo ageretswe ku mazina kandi iciyumviro atomora ni ico kubaza. Tuyita insigarirazina-ngereka kabaza.
Umwimenyerezo
Hindura aya mungane muri kabaza ukoresheje insigarirazina-ngereka kabaza wongere werekane n'iyo ari yo.
