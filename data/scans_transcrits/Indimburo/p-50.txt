- Oya ni ko ntiyari kubishobora.
- Koko urya mupira wari uryoshe !
d. Ca akarongo ku ntumbuzi z'uguhakana
- Oya sinamubonye.
- Eka data biraremereye sinobiterura.
- None wari kumwe na barya bafashwe ?
- Bakugaburiye ? Haba namba.
- Na we wacerewe ? Ihibambewe sinigera ncererwa.
- Eka da, ntiyakomeretse, yaguye gusa.
- Ishwi data sinzogenda abavyeyi batandungitse.
- Oya sindaruha !
e. Erekana intumbuzi z'igihe ziri muri ayo mungane
- Kera isi yaramera cane kuruta ubu.
- Yagiye kera cane sinzi iyo yaroye.
- Abo hanyuma bazosanga twarateye imbere.
- Yagiye n'ijoro kandi azogaruka atevye.
- Abo hambere baba mu nzu z'ivyatsi.
- Rindira turagenda handi hato.
- Abaje ubu nibasubire inyuma, bazogaruka ejo.
- Ni murorere gatoyi araje ubu nyene.
- Twahuye ejo bundi avuga ko azoza vuba.
- Muzohora na ntaryo muzana amasuka kuko tugiye gutangura vuba kwiga kurima.
2.4. Gatangazi
Uturorero :
1. Ororowe, arababaye !
2. Hambewe, yatonekaye aho yari yarakomeretse !
3. Mamawe, washenye nyinshi kibondo !
4. Eheeee, Samandari arafashwe !
5. Agweeee, nabivuga akavyemera !
Ayo mungane yose afise iciyumviro c'uko nyene kuvuga atangaye. Aha hose ayo mungane atangurwa n'akajambo k'ugutangara. Ako kajambo kitwa Gatangazi. Gatanganzi ni akajambo k'ukwitangaza kerekana ko nyene kuvuga ashimye canke agaye ikintu bakoreye imbere yiwe canke yumva mu mutwe. Utundi tujambo twa Gatangazi ni utu :
eee !, ududuwe !, aha !, yuu !, aho ! (mu kwitabira), emwe gaye !, itu !, ituye !, bacobomwe !, da !, data we !, yororowe !, ashiii !, aho wa !...
Imyimenyerezo
Tora akajambo ka gatangazi kari muri aya mungane
- Ohoo, raba ingene biyereka neza !
- Yuu, Kadoyi araguye !
- Ororowe, uwuri aho araberewe !
- Eeee, erega bansize !
- Ashiii, ndaruhutse rero !
- Egwe ma, ko wari umubonye wamuva inyuma !
- Ohoo, n'iyo ampenda ntituramukanye !
- Yuu, wagiye mu bantu wambaye gutyo !
- Emwe gaye, mbega ivy'iri ci tuzobikira !
- Itu, Bigeni yashaye !
2.5. Inkebuzo
Uturorero
- Ewe mugabo, kenyera unyumvirize !
- Mbe hewe, nta co wondusha !
- Erega, uwokora iciza yocuhira !
- Mbeye, utona yaravyemeye !
