2.3. Intumbuzi
- Nasezeye umusezero kera n'ubu nturashanyuka.
- Aho hepfo haciye impehe nsa.
- Genda buhoro nta co uca ku mahembe.
- Afise amaganya menshi cane ntashobora kumererwa neza.
- Urazogaruka ejo.
- Reka gufata gutyo rero uwo muhoro uhava wikomeretsa[?].
- Bamufashe yivye hanyuma baja kumupfunga.
Intumbuzi ni utujambo tudahinduka dutumbura[?] iciyumviro kiri mu rivuga, mu ngereka, mu nsigarirazina[?] canke mu yindi ntumbuzi. Intumbuzi ntikama ari akajambo[?] kirebanga. Ishobora kugirwa n'akajambo kiretsenga[?]. Ishobora kugirwa n'akajambo kamwe (neza) tubiri canke[?] turenga (aho hepfo, mu nyuma y'aho,...).
Amoko y'intumbuzi
Hari ho intumbuzi z'ukugene, intumbuzi z'ukubaza, intumbuzi z'ukwemera, intumbuzi z'uguhakana.
2.3.1. Intumbuzi z'ukugene
Uturorero
1. Asa neza uyu munsi.
2. Arandara nabinabi, bidashemeye.
3. Arakomeye cane ngo ganuka.
4. Agenda buhorobuhoro sinzi ko ashika.
5. Yabigize nkana.
Intumbuzi y'ukugene itumbura iciyumviro mu kwerekana uburyo canke ubugene igikorwa kigirwa.
Nko muri utwo turorero, hari mwo ukugene amagara y'uwo bavuga ameze, uburyo agenda, ingene yabigize...
Izindi ntumbuzi ni nk'izi :
bukebuke, n'impaka, neza na neza, ningoga, rwose, imbonankubone, gahorogahoro, gusa, canecane, buhoro, icese.
2.3.2. Intumbuzi z'igihe
Uturorero :
1. Nta gakuru kiwe nzi duherukana kera.
2. Tuzosubira kubonana hanyuma.
3. Yagiye hakiri kare.
4. Urwaruka rwa none rurakerebutse.
5. Ejo twarabonanye.
Intumbuzi z'igihe zitumbura iciyumviro kiri mu ryungane zikerekana igihe igikorwa kiri muri iryo ryungane kiberako.
Muragabe
Intumbuzi z'igihe zirashobora gusa n'intumbuzi z'ikibanza. Mu kuzitandukanya rero umuntu afatira ku nsiguro iri mu ryungane.
Akarorero
- Genda wicare imbere (intumbuzi y'ikibanza).
- Turabonana imbere y'isaha zitandatu (Intumbuzi y'igihe).
- Inyuma ya Ntare wa gatatu nta mwami yasubiye gutwara (Intumbuzi y'igihe).
- Biduda agiye guca ibigori inyuma y'inzu (Intumbuzi y'ikibanza).
Izindi ntumbuzi z'igihe ni izi :
vuba, kare, hambere, buno nyene, bugingo, na kera na rindi, kera na vuba, kare na kare, rimwe na rimwe, ubu, ubu nyene, ubu bwajisho, mu nyuma, na mwa mugoroba,
