Muramenye :
Imigwi imwimwe ntitandukanya rudende na bwinshi.
- "inka" yaba imwe canke nyinshi tuguma tuvuga "inka".
- umugwi wa 14 kubera uri mwo amazina avuga ibintu bitaboneka ntitwerekana rudende na bwinshi.
Akarorero : ubutwari, ubugabo, ubwenge...,
- umugwi wa 16 na wo nyene ni kwa kundi ntutandukanya rudende na bwinshi ; "ahantu" haba hamwe canke harenga tuguma tuvuga " ahantu".
Imigwi imwimwe mu kwerekana ubwinshi itira iyindi migwi :
- uwa 11 utira uwa 10 ; akarorero : urukwavu - inkwavu
- uwa 15 utira uwa 6 ; ak. : ukuboko - amaboko
- uwa 14 utira uwa 6 ; ak. : ubwato - amato.
2.3.3. Itsitso
