Akanyoni katagurutse ntikamenya iyo bweze
Niyongere yararindiriye inyishu ya rya kete, igihe kitari gito. Amaze kuyironka aca atangura gukora.
Umusi umwe yari agodotse, ahura na Bayaga avuye gutembera, Bayaga aca amwiganira ingene Butoyi yagiye mu mahanga : « Aho Butoyi amariye gutunga, yaciye yiyumvira kuja gutembera mu bindi bihugu. Umusi uri izina, yaritoye agenda i Bujumbura gusaba impapuro zo kuja mu mahanga. Azironse, aca abaza umusi ubwato buzogira i Kigoma, ariyandikisha. Imisi ibiri iheze, yurira ubwato. Ashitse i Kigoma, araraho. Bukeye abandanya urugendo rwiwe mw'igari ya moshi », imushikana i Dar-Es-Salaam. Aho hose yari kumwe n'umugenzi amaze gutembera henshi. Baragiye mu mangazini zaho, barabona utuntu tutari duke. Barateba mbere baja kuraba ibahari yitwa Indiyano. Ese ivyiza bahabonye !
Haciye imisi, bagenda muri Uganda, bashikira mu gisagara gikuru citwa Kampala. Bahamara uruhusha. Bahavuye, bafashe indege, ibajana ku mugwa mukuru w'igihugu ca Kenya witwa Nayirobi. Aho baca hose, barinezereza uko bashoboye kwose. Ururimi bavuga gusumba izindi zose cari igiswahiri. Emwe urwo rugendo rwarabagiriye akamaro kanini ! »
Niyongere amubwira ati :
– Ewe Bayaga uramenya inkuru, ivyo vyose wabimenye gute ?
– Ni Butoyi yabinyandikiye. N'ubu aheruka kumbwira ko aho i Nayirobi, yaharonse umuntu azohora amurungikira ibidandazwa. Ngo aho baca hose, Butoyi yagenda araraba ingene abandi badandaza. Aho Butoyi ahindukiriye, yaciye asaba uruhusha rwo kurangurira mu mahanga. Agira imana bararumuha.
