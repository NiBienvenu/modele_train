Urudandaza rwa Butoyi rwateye imbere
Ku musi wa gatandatu Bayaga ntiyasubira kw'ishure. Yaciye ajana na mugenziwe kumesura impuzu, bagenda baraganira. Bayaga amubaza ati :
– Wambwiye ngo Butoyi afise ibidandazwa vyinshi, none burya araronka aho abikwiza muri ya ndaro yiwe ? Uwundi amwishura ati :
– Yahavuye yubaka inzu y'idandarizo. Bwa bundi yarashishikaye kudandaza, abonye amafaranga agwiriye, aca arubaka. Iyo nzu yayineze iruhande y'amahwane y'amabarabara ari hepfo y'iwabo. Aca yimuriramwo ibidandazwa vyose yari afise : amasabuni yo kwiyoga n'ukumesa, inzembe, amatara, ipeterori, itabi, ibibiriti, n'ibindi vyinshi abona ko bikenewe. Butoyi yaciye arondera umuntu amufasha kudandaza.
Ubona ko Bayaga adashirwa, abaza wa mubanyi wiwe ati :
– Mbe Butoyi atwara gute ivyo aranguye ? Uri uwundi ati :
– Yabonye ko abagurira iwe babaye benshi, atagishobora kuzana ku mutwe ibikwiye, aca agura ikinga. Haciye imisi arigenderako, yumva ritanguye kuguma mu bikanyagisho, aribaza ico rirwaye arakiyoberwa. Ni ko kuja kubaza Mvuyekure, aca amwishura ati :
– Uragura amavuta yaryo uzoje urayashira mu bikanyagisho, mu munyororo, mu gitako, no mu mahembe yaryo. Uzoja uramara nk'imisi itari mike, wongere ushiremwo ayandi.
– Ni ryatoboka hoho nzobigenza gute ?
– Ko utaramenya ingene barihoma, urampamagara ndabikwereke. Ikindi wokwitwararika, ni ukwama uraba ko impemu zikwiye, kandi ntuzotirigane ngo urigendereko mw'ijoro ata tara rifise.
