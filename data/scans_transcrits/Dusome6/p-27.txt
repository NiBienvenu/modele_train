Insiguro y'amajambo agoye
Ivyahi : ibisaswa vy'uruyoya.
Umwaritsi : umwigeme afasha uwavyaye akiri mu kiriri.
Indeshi : inzoga canke ivyokurya bazanira uwavyaye.
Ntarye urubutiro : ntasonzere mu kiriri.
Urukariha : akabugita bakarisha bakagakoresha mu kumwa. Ivyitiranwa vyarwo : urugongo, intakare.
Barosa (kwosa) : bafata udushitsi tumota bakadushira mu kantu kameze nk'akabehe canke ku rujo bakaduturira. Umwotsi watwo ni wo wamotesha ivyambarwa.
Somambike : abakunzi bashika ku mutima.
Kwema : guhagarara warikanuye.
