Ico utazi urabaza
Umusi umwe, Bayaga yari aragiranye na bagenzibe, baba baratera inkuru abiganira ivyo yiga mw'ishure, na bo bamuyagira ingene ibihe vy'umusi bihita. Umwe muri bo ati :
– Inka ziturutse bahita mu maturuka. Izuba rigamburuye, inyana ntonto zica zitaha. Ico gihe haba ari ku musase. Mu mashoka, izuba riba rikaze cane, rica ibibatsi, vya mpingwe biduma mu kirere. Ico gihe abantu baragodoka, bakaja kwihemba, haba ari ku mutaga. Inka zihodotse, ziravyagira, zikavyaguruka ku muhingamo. Inyana zisubira kurisha mu masohoka, izuba riba rihumvye. Bayaze kera, riba rirarenze baracura.
Uwundi musi, Bayaga na benewabo basohora inyana, bazahurira mu kibara, bashinga urukiramende, barasimba barushanywa. Ku kirengazuba, aho za nyana zigire zone umurima w'ibiharage, Bamire yari yemeye inka ku muvumba, arabahamagara, ababwira ngo bazigarure, bace bazicura. Bo bagira ngo haracari kare, bari bahenzwe n'igihendabaja. Bayaga we, aho gusiriha gusa, yari yaciye ibicaniro, acura avyikoreye, aca asubira inyuma gutegera inka. Ico gihe, yumva uwundi mwungere acuye yiyamiriza ati :
Yoo ! Tambuka tugende Mugamba wa Bitambuka imbundazi
Ehee, shira imbere nk'umuzinga nyagushira ishavu ngashira irungu
Hwiii !
Ehe data w'inka we !
Nzobajana mu bisozi bitotahaye, abungere bakamenya kuharagira
Mu Rutongangiramuyonga iwabo wa Mujandi na Jambo !
Data ga mwaranjanye kwaka mungarukana kweya.
Mwarambujije gusenya n'ukuvoma mwa ngirakimazi mwe.
Hogi, hogi, hogi, hogi, hwiiii hwiiii !
Mu mugarira, Bayaga ararahura, aracanira akotsi gafata ijuru. Amakama ageze, arekura inyana, nyina na we ahereza amazi senaka
