Nta wurya akatamugoye
Umusi umwe Bayaga yari avuye kw'ishure, abona Butoyi agendera mu muduga mushasha. Ageze imbere, ahwana na wa mubanyi wiwe, amubaza ingene Butoyi yaronse iyo modokari.
Na we arabimwiganira kuko yari incuti ya Butoyi, akaba abizi ati :
– Igihe kimwe Butoyi yumvise arushe cane avuye kurangura, aca yibaza ati : « Mbe ko mfise amafaranga nabitse, ubwo nogenda mw'isandugu ry'ukuziganya, ntibongurana nkagura imodokari izoza iratwara ivyo ndanguye, aha nirirwa ndacika umugongo ndabisehesha ikinga ? »
Bukeye yandikira umuyobozi mukuru w'isandugu ry'ukuziganya. Imisi mikeyi iheze, aramwishura, amubwira ko amwemereye. Butoyi abonye iyo nyishu, ntiyatevye kuja kuyatora, yongereza ya yandi yari afise, aca agura imodoka.
Butoyi abonye ko urudandaza rwiwe rutera ruja imbere, yigira inama yo kwubaka inzu y'ikizungu azodandarizamwo.
Atangura kurondera ihumbi : amatafari, umusenyi, amabuye, isima, amabati n'ibindi... Arigwijije, atangura kwubaka. Iyo nzu ayigaburamwo ibihimba bitatu : ico azodandarizamwo, ico azoshinguramwo ivyo aranguye n'ikindi c'urunywero. Nya nzu ntiyatevye kwuzura, yimuriramwo vya bidandazwa, yongera ayiranguriramwo ikawa. Iyo kawa yaramwungukiye cane, arishura n'umwenda yari asigaraniye isandugu ry'ukuziganya. »
Mu misi ikurikira, Butoyi ahura na Bayaga avuye kw'ishure, aramwunguruza, bagenda baraganira inzira yose, amubaza ati :
– Mbega uyu muduga wavuye muri rwa rudandaza gusa ? Butoyi amwishura ati :
– Egome, narashishikaye kudandaza, ndaronka ibiciye aka kaduga. Kandi narubatse inzu nshasha y'idandarizo. Ubu na ho ndiko ndubaka inzu nzobamwo.
Bayaga avyumvise ni ho yabona ko n'aho Butoyi atabandanije amashure yashoboye kwibeshaho mu buzima kubera urudandaza rwiwe rwagumye rutera imbere.
