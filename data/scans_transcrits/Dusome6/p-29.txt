Kera barya umwaka bate ?
Umusi uri izina, Bayaga yariko arataha avuye kw'ishure, asanga umutama Ndayirukiye mu nzira. Baba barafatanije urugendo. Bashitse iruhande y'umurima w'uburo, nya mukurambere ati :
– Aho hambere hahise uburo bwari agahambaye ; ni bwo bakoresha mu kurya umwaka. Bayaga na we ati :
– Barya umwaka bate ? Nya mutama arabimwiganira ati :
– Imbere yo kurya umwaka, barindira ko ukwezi kwa Rusama, ari kwo Rwirabura, guhera. Muri ukwo kwezi, nta warya umwaka kandi nta wamara urubanza, eka n'ibisuma ntivyiba. Abarundi biyumvira ko uwobirengako yohumirwa. Umwaka bakunda kuwurya mu kwezi kwa Ruheshi kuko henshi uburo bwaba bweze.
Iyo bagomba kurya umwaka, baritegura. Umusi wo kuwurya, baba bateguye ifu y'uburo. Ico batibagira kandi, yari amavuta y'ukurunga imboga : inyabutongo, isogi, isogo, umusoma n'izindi... Inkono, inko inkoko, umudahara, igisabiko ba-ba bavyogeje. Inzoga y'amasaka yaba yateguwe. Kuri uwo musi baririnda gutongana n'umuntu uwo ari we wese. Hageze ku mugoroba, inarugo yaracumba, agahakura irobe rya senaka n'imyando y'abana, agashira aho. Ubwo kuryako umwaka, yaba yabukumiye mu ruhande rumwe rw'inkono. Yabuhakura ubwa nyuma, akabuzana ku mudahara, agahereza senaka. Akakira, akabega intore, akatiramwo imboga, agatamira, agaca avuga ati : « Umwaka mwiza, uwawuntegeyemwo arakawugwamwo. » Yaca abega iyindi ntore, agahereza umugore, akamubwira ati : « Akira umwaka, uwawugutegeyemwo arakawugwamwo. » Imfura ica ikurikira, se akongera akagira kwa kundi, ubuheta bugakurikira gushika ku muhererezi. Umwana atari i muhira n'utazi kurya nyina ni we yawubarira. Uwumaze kurya na we nyina yaramutamika. Bose bakwiwe, baca basoma kuri ya nzoga y'amasaka. Hatangura senaka, hagasubira inanaka na we agahereza imfura, gutyo gutyo, bose bagahetura.
