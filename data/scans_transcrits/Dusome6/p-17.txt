Butoyi arashima impanuro za Mvuyekure, barasezerana arataha.
Iryo kinga ryaramufashije kuzana ibintu vyinshi, ya nzu irateba[?] iranihira[?].
Iyo nkuru igeze aho, baranura za mpuzu bameshe, barataha, baja kuzigorora.
Ibintu vyarayogeye
Haciye ukwezi Bayaga atabonana na wa mubanyi, kuko avuye kw'ishure yashikira mu dukorwa tw'i muhira. Aho atanguriye uburuhuko bw'igice ca mbere c'umwaka, ni ho bahura baba baraganira. Wa mubanyi aca amwiganira amakuba yashikiye Butoyi ati :
– Ejo bundi Butoyi yari aramutse aharura ibintu yagurishije n'ibisigaye. Aravye amafaranga amaze kuronka asanga yarahomvye. Asubira guharura kimwekimwe cose, abona baramwivye amasabuni atari make, imifuko y'isukari n'ibirahuri vyinshi.
Yakura wa mukozi amubwira ati :
– Zana ningoga amafaranga yanje, ugarukane n'ibindi vyose wivye, utaribonerako. Wa mugabo abanza kurahira yanana ko atigeze amenya iyo vyaroye. Ariko hari hakiri mu maguru masha, ibintu ntiyari bwabigurishe. Butoyi aramukambiriza nyabuna ati :
– Urahira ubesha, utabizanye ubu bwa vuba, uzobizana vyanse bikunze.
Erega ca gisuma kibonye ko rukamvye, ryakanutse, kibarira Butoyi ko kigiye kubirondera, na we akirungikana abashingantahe babiri ngo ntigihave gitoroka. Kiragenda, kinyegurura vya bintu vyose, kirabizana. Ico kitamusubije ni amafaranga kuko cari camaze kuyakoresha. Butoyi agishira imbere y'abagabo, bagicira urubanza rwo kuzoyariha mu mezi atatu, kitayaronse, bakagipfungisha.
Butoyi yaciye arondera uwundi mukozi. Abanza kumuca amahani ati :
– Uraba udakorakora ahandi ho uzokabona.
