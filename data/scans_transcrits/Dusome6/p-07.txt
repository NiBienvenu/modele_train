Ntavyibuha ubuvyimbe nk'ingumba
Ngo Bitaho vy'ugutahana vya Sebatoni
Mbaaaaa
Se wa Bayaga agejeje aho, inkono iba irahiye, barafungura.
Insiguro y'amajambo agoye
Impingwe : inzuki zitagira imboyi, ziduma mu kirere canecane mu gihe c'amashoka y'inka.
Guhodoka : ni harya inka zihejeje kunywa amazi, zihaze, inyota iheze.
Kuvyagira : ni harya inka ziruhuka zihodotse, zimwe ziraryama izindi zikaguma zihagaze.
Ku muhingamo : ni hagati y'umurango n'umugoroba.
Mu masohoka : igihe inyana zisubira kurisha. Haba ari nk'isaha y'icenda.
Guhumba (izuba) : harya izuba ritakibabaza, ritagikaze.
Kwahura inyana mu kibara : kuzirekurira ahantu hari ubwatsi buremvye hahoze umurima.
Kwemera inka ku muvumba : kuragira inka ahegereye imirima.
Igihendabaja : ni harya akazuba kaka rigira rirenge ukama ugira ngo haracari kare.
Gusiriha : gukina cane (bivugwa ku bana).
Ibicaniro : ivyatsi bakoresha mu gucanira inka.
Mu mugarira : ni igihe bugarira irembo inka zatashe.
Kweya : guca ibibatsi, kwakaka.
Amakama : igihe c'ugukama.
Kwizigura : gukaraba.
Injishi : ni umugozi utsivye babohesha inka ku maguru iyo bagomba kuyikama. Babigira ku nka igora gukama, itera.
Icakunze (icansi) : ni igikoresho bakamiramwo amata, kandi bakakiyanywesha.
Guhaza : gukama.
Kubonekesha : kuvugira amazina inka ziriko ziranywa amazi.
Amashinga (yose) : intara, ibihugu.
Rukiga, Muganza : amazina y'imisozi.
Igisabo kitagira umurando : igisabo kitagira imigaga.
Kuranda : kubarira ahasadutse (urwato, umukuza, igisabo...)
Taba ritavye imigongo : inka ivyibushe, uruti rw'umugongo ntiruboneka.
Kuvyibuha ubuvyimbe : kuvyimbagana ubitewe n'indwara abakubona bakagira ngo uravyibushe.
Igisangaza : ikivumvu kinini cane, igishwaburira kijishwe mu migano canke mu mivumu.
Guterwa imibungo : guterwa iteka.
Guhweza imikurira : gucutsa inyana.
