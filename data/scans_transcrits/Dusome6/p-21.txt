Babitarura bate ?
Umusi umwe imvura yariko iragwa, Bayaga avanye na sewabo Kamana mu kaguriro. Bagize gutya babona imodokari ije iva hakurya y'uruzi, Bayaga ati :
– Irya modokari iza guca he ?
– Harya hepfo hariho ikiraro.
– Mbe bagitarura bate ?
– Ewe ni ivy'abahinga ! Bahengera mu ci, amazi amaze kugabanuka, bakaja mw'ishamba guca ibiti vy'imisave canke vy'imivugangoma. Ubu bateye imika, amasederi, ubujwari ni vyo bakoresha. Bahengera bikavye, bagahana isango bakaja kubiseha. Imbere yo kubiseha, babanza kubiringaniza, bakabitobora ku mitwe, bakavyambika imigozi babikwegesha, gushika ku ruzi. Ico gihe vyitwa imigogo. Bamwe baca boga, bakajabuka hakurya bafashe ya migozi. Bageze ku nkombe yo hakurya, bagakwega ya migozi iboheye ku migogo, abo hakuno na bo baba bariko barasunika uwo mugogo ngo ujabuke. Bagira gutyo no ku yindi. Bahejeje, baca bafata isaso bakaranda hejuru, kurya babarata inzu, igitarurwa kikaba cuzuye. Ku bitarurwa bitobito vyo ku mabarabara, baca barenzako ivu.
Ibiraro vy'ubu bicako imiduga iremereye, babitaruza amabuye n'ibito. Hari na hamwehamwe basigaye bataruza ivyuma, babishira canecane ku nzuzi nininini.
