Umuhondo w'amahonda : Inzoga banywa mu masaka baciye ubwa[?] mbere.
Imvumba : imiganda y'ubwishaza.
Guhomera : gushira ivyatsi vyumvye hejuru y'ibitiba vyuzuye ubwishaza, bagahoma n'amase.
Umudidizo : uburyo bwo gusekura ku rugero.
Nta muntu ari umwete nka Butoyi
Umusi umwe, Bayaga yahuye na Butoyi avuye kurangura bararamukanya. Baherukana baciga hamwe, umwe ari mu mwaka wa mbere, uwundi mu wa gatandatu. Bayaga yarihuta cane ntiyashoboye kubaza Butoyi ingene yatanguye kudandaza.
Ageze imbere ahamvya umubanyi wa Butoyi, arabimubaza. Uri uwundi abimugira ku muriri ati :
– Butoyi yageze mu wa gatandatu biramugora, ikibazo co kwungururizwa mu w'indwi kiramunanira. Aca yigira inama y'ukwitunga mu buzima bwiwe, atabanje kwirirwa arata wakuba ngo arondere akazi aho atari bukabone.
Aho yari akiri mw'ishure, yari yarize uburyo bwiza bw'ukurima. Bararimye, birakunda biraza, baravyimbura, barabigurisha, bashinga ishirahamwe ry'ukudandaza, aca amenyeraho n'ugucuruza. Aho aviriye mw'ishure asaba se amafaranga makeyi yo gutangura kudandaza. Ahera ku bintu biciriye hagufi : utubiriti, itabi, udusabuni, n'ibindi...
Umusi uri izina, yafashe umufuko agenda mw'isoko. Agira n'imana iryo soko ni ryo ryarimwo ibintu bizimbutse mu masoko yose yo hafi y'iwabo. Ashitseyo, agura ibidandazwa bishobora kumwungukira ningoga na ningoga, kandi ababanyi baca bagura badatevye. Amasaka ni yo yabonye ko yomwungukira. Butoyi ashitse i muhira araruhuka, ahava agenda gutemberera ababanyi, ababwira ko yaranguye amahonda. Bukeye, benshi baraza kuyagura canecane abafise imanza zo guterera n'abagomba kwenga inzoga z'ukudandaza. Yarabandanije kubirangura kuko yabibonamwo inyungu. Abonye ko amaze kuronka amafaranga menshi arashishikara kudandaza : umuceri, umunyu, ibiharage, itabi, ibibiriti n'ibindi. Aho hose yagwa epfo na ruguru, yashaka kwubaka inzu azodandarizamwo.
