Iyimbura rirageze
Bukeye Bayaga ajana na nyina gusora ibiyoba. Bigwiriye, Bayaga na benewabo bandura ivyo bamaze gusoroma, babitoborera mu bivumvu. Bahejeje, babitwara i muhira, bimwe barabironga, babitekana ibishishwa, ibindi baravyanikira. Bahengereye vyumye, bimwe barabishoye, ibindi barabibika ngo bazoje barabigiramwo intotera canke babirye inkarange.
Muri ico gihe nyene, ibiharage biri mu kabande vyari bigeze kurandurwa. Mu gitondo, bazindutse kubisarura. Bageze i muhira bacacura imitobe barayitonora, barateka. Ibisigaye babishisha ku rugo no mu kigo. Bahengereye vyumye, barabikubita, barabigosora, bavyimburira mu bitiba. Haheze imisi, amasaka yari munsi y'urugo, aba areze. Se wa Bayaga arondera abamufasha, bazinduka kuyatema. Abana na bo barayandura, bayajana aho bariko baratonda umurara. Uwo murara uguma ngaho gushika amasaka yume. Yahamaze nk'ukwezi hanyuma barayagenya, bayimburira mu bigega.
Ivyo birangiye barayasekura, banywa umuhondo w'amahonda.
Kenshi iyo bariko barasekura, baravugira isekuro ngo bimare ubute.
