II. Uri aho Runyenyeri wari wambaye
Icitabirizo : Uraho Runyenyeri wari wambaye !
1. Wiriwe nzira y'inka yego wiriwe
Yuzuriza abagenzi wari wambaye
2. Uri aho kimararungu yego wiriwe
Umutuhura abandi wari wambaye
Runyenyeri ni rwiza wari wambaye
3. Umurangwa ruhimbi yego wiriwe
Umukura agahinda wari wambaye
4. Umusekura mutwenzi yego wiriwe
Impundu igasasira wari wambaye
Runyenyeri ni rwiza wari wambaye
5. Uranyereka umuhora yego wiriwe
Ucura Ngoma na Maza wari wambaye
6. Ni hariya kw'itaba yego wiriwe
Mu bahana kiyago wari wambaye
Runyenyeri ni rwiza wari wambaye
7. Hejuru y'uruzi yego wiriwe
Rwuzura rudatwara wari wambaye
8. Nzoza mu gatasi yego wiriwe
Kadatanga inyana gutaha wari wambaye
Runyenyeri ni rwiza wari wambaye
9. Hejuru y'imivumba yego wiriwe
Bavyukiriza guhaza wari wambaye
10. Mfise Murwa na Birenzi yego wiriwe
Na Gitare co guhonga wari wambaye
11. Runyenyeri ni rwiza yego wiriwe
Ushatse azorutamba wari wambaye
Runyenyeri ni rwiza wari wambaye
