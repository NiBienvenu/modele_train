Mu buruhuko, Bayaga yaragiye kuramutsa inasenge asanga bariko barashwabura ivyo barimye.
Bukeye, Bayaga yazindukanye n'abandi bana kurinda inyoni mu ngano. Izuba rigamburuye, inyoni zigabanutse, Bayaga na ba bana baciye baja gufasha abandi guseha ubwishaza, babujana ku mbuga. Umurango uramye, bari bamaze kuburangiriza ku mbuga, bagerekeranya imiganda[?]. Mu kubukubita, bafashe ibibando ari bane, bashira ku mbuga imvumba zine, hanyuma baja barakuranwa, babiribabiri bakubitira rimwe, gushika bunoge. Bunoze, barakunkumuye ibishazashaza baraba ko ubwishaza bwahezemwo, baca babijana kubirunda hirya y'imbuga. Bazana ubundi bwishaza, barabukubita kwa kundi gushika baburangize. Baca bafata intaro baragosora, babusuka mu biseke. Babushikanye i muhira, babugabanyako ubwo guteka n'ubwo gushora, ubw'imbuto babuhomera mu bitiba.
Mu kwezi kwa Myandagaro bimbuye amateke, bagenya n'ingano.
Insiguro y'amajambo agoye
Gusora : gusoromesha isuka ibiyoba.
Intotera : ifu y'ibiyoba bashigisha igaheza ikaba inkozo.
Imitobe : ibiharage vyo ku mitwetwe bikiri bitoto, bitarahishira.
Gushisha : kumanika ibiharage ku rugo.
Igitiba : akagega gatoyi bashingura mu nzu, bakakimburiramwo.
Gutonda umurara : gutondesha amasaka aho azokwumira.
