Ivomo
Ivomo rirafise akamaro kanini cane. Ni co gituma turikorera neza kugira imicafu ibe mike, amazi agume ari meza. Aho tuvoma, kera abana barahakinira, bakaharagira, turabiraba duca tuhubakira urugo. Ubu ni heza cane n'abakuru bacu baranezerewe, baranadusaba kuzoguma uko mu kugirira isuku aho tuvoma.
Amazi
Amazi arafise akamaro kanini cane. Umubiri wacu ukeneye amazi atari make. Akamaro k'amazi si ako gusa. Turayakenera mu guteka, mu kumesura no mu kugirira isuku aho tuba. Bana, gerageza rero gufata neza amasoko yose mubona, amazi yame ari meza, murabona ko tuyazeyeko uburaro n'uburamuko.
Ugucuruza
Datawacu ni umucuruza akomeye cane. Yama atugurana ibiharage n'umuceri tukazobimuriha haciye igihe kitari gito. Aracuruza ibigori, ifu, isukari, amata, amatara, amasuka,... maze bikamuzanira amahera atari make. Iwe harakaka : hari ho n'amatara, yaraguze amamodoka, amameza, amatebe, ububati, uburiri, eka ni akaroruhore.
