Uwutarima ntamira
Barancira yabariye nyina ati : "Ncira umugani ma." Nyina ati : "Uwutarima ntamira." Barancira awumvise, amara akanya gatoyi yiyumvira. Erega ntiyahagaze, yaciye afata isuka, ahitana murumunawe baja kurima icibare cabo. Imvura imaze gutasura, bahatera ibiharage. None ntiworaba ingene uwo murima uhimbaye. Ugiye kubavuna ubukene : bazoronka ico bafungura, baronke amafaranga n'agahuzu. None uwareze amaboko we azobikika gute ?
