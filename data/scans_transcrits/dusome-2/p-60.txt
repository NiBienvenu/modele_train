Ndabikika gute ?
Ese isuka y'abandi ! Nkwirwa hehe ga bane ba mama ? Nagize ngo ndacayikubita hasi, isigara mw'isemo, nanje nsigarana igice c'umuhini mu ntoke.
Ntako yari nyayo, umuhini wayo wari warabunzwe. Mbega ingene mama yayikunda, ndamuja hehe ? Ni ayikunde yari ikarishe kandi yari nshasha. Bayiguze mwakuyu, ariko n'ubu ubugi bwayo umenga ni urwembe.
Ko mbona ibiti vy'ubu bigoye, umuhini mwiza azowukura hehe ? Ese ugufyina kwanje yemwe ! Ko nari mfise agafuni, ubona naja kwihanga umujojo w'abandi kuki ?
Bontabara bakangirira ikigongwe, sinoba ngisubira kwiha ivyo batampaye.
