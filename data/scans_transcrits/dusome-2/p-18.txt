Icirore
Madebari yibonye mu cirore kimanitse ku ruhome. Ahamagara nyina atwenga ati : «Ma, ndabona uwundi mwana mu cirore, sinzi iyo avuye.» Nyina amusubiza ati : «Genda ube uramukinisha.» Madebari atangura kumukora mu maso, amusokoza, amutwengesha. Yatevye, aribaza igituma uyo mwana amwigana mu two agize twose. Madebari biramushavuza, aca asaba ngo bamumwirukanire. Nyina areguka, aratwenga. Amubwira ati : «Erega naho bigutera umwikomo, urya mwana si uwundi ni wewe wibonye.»
Madebari ngo avyumve aratwenga arakumbagara n'imbavu ziruma ; ariko ntiyabitegereye nka neza.
