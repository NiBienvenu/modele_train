Kariwabo arahaze
Ng'uwo Kariwabo avuye kw'ishure. Nyina amwakirije yompi n'igishika c'umuvyeyi. Yinjiye mu nzu yakiriye isahani amushiriye imfungurwa. Kariwabo abibonye amaganya aramwuzura amubwira ati :
- Ma, sinshaka kurya ndarwaye.
- Ubabara he ga mwananje ?
- Mbabara mu nda.
- Mbega Kariwabo ko mbona uhaze cane wariye iki ?
- Oya nta co nariye ngira ndwaye inzoka.
- Ubwo ga Kariwabo ntumbesha ?
Ko waheruka kunywa umuti, izo nzoka zikurya zavuye hehe ?
Ubwo ntugomba kumpisha ko wariye mu gahinga ?
Kariwabo arateterwa amubarira ati : «Navuye kwiga nca aho Kayoya aragiye, nsanga ibijumbu yokeje birahiye, turabisangira. Ntiwokwumva ingene vyari biryoshe.»
- Aho nakuburije, wasubiriye kurya ivyo mu gahinga ? Urazi iyo vyavuye ? Ubwo si ivyibano ?
- Oya ma, urazi ko Kayoya atiba, ni ivyo yakuye mu cibare ciwe.
