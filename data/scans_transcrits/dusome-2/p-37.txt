Amero na yo ni indwara !
Muringanira ni umukecuru w'amarara. Umunsi umwe, yumvise aho babaze, agenda ariruka, asanga inyama nzizanziza bazimaze. Avuvudikira ibikoba.
Ashitse i muhira, abiterera ku ziko, arengerako aracanira, ugusha mambu ! Vyakabaye kera araheba abiha amenyo, araguye igikoba kimwe arajigita, ngo ashikure ashaka gutemba. Ntiyashirwa, arasubira, noneho kimuta ku nkingi.
Aho yohevye, bimwanka mu nda, apfa kukimira, kiramuniga. Agira ishavu araheba ati : «Nakurya ntitwarwana. Ndahatswe nari mpwereye.»
