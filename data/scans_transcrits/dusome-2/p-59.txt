Ibiyoba biraryoha !
Umunsi umwe, mwene mamawacu yaje atekeye ibiyoba kw'ishure. Arampa mbanza kwanka, kuko ntari bwabibone. Aca afata ikiyoba, aragitonora, atamira urutete rumwe ; urundi ararumpa ngo numvirize. Ndahumiriza ndaruhekenya, numva ruraryoshe.
Duca turatonora ivyari bisigaye, turabisangira ; mbere vyinshi ni je nabiriye. Abonye ko bindyoheye, anyemerera ko azonzanira ibindi hanyuma. Araheza arantumira ngo nzoje kubirya mu buruhuko. Na bwo bwari bwegereje !
Ngo mpengere burashitse, nsaba uruhusha ngo nzoje kubaramutsa. Ariko agatima kose kari kuri vya biyoba. Hageze kugenda, mama arandumaniriza inkoko, mukurwanje aramperekeza. Dushitseyo, duhwanira na mamawacu kw'irembo avuye kubisoroma ; akanyamuneza karansya. Aca yihinda mu nzu, afata inkono arakaranga, aba ari vyo atuzimana.
Iminsi twahamaze, nta na rimwe twasivye ibiyoba.
Eka twatashe turyimaze, mbere n'ukubishaka tutakibishaka.
