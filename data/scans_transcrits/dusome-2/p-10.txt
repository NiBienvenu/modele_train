Kuragira inka
I muhira iwacu turafise inka zitari nke.
Bugica, Kabura akorokana inkuyo*, akazihanagura, akazishitura. Akazuba kamaze gufata, data aca arekura inyana agakama. Imbere yo guturutsa, mama aragaburira Kabura. Ahejeje, data aramutambutsa, gushika amushikirize abo baragirana. Mutsama* ni yo igenda izirongoye. Ku musase, ndagenda kumusigaranira, kugira ngo ashore inyana, azicure, yongere aje gufungura. Ahindukira kunkura mu masohoka y'inyana.
inkuyo : ivyatsi, ikiremo canke ikindi kintu cose bahanaguza inka kugira bazigirire isuku
Mutsama : izina bita inka
