Aririwe..., ndatsitaye
- Nzeyimana..., urizwa n'iki ?
- Ndatsitaye.
- Utsitaye ku ki ?
- Mbona ari ikibuye. Mbega yemwe ni nde yakinteze ?
Ubwo iri buye ntiryari mu yo mwamye mushigikije urusyo ?
- Ko twari twayabitse kure, iryo ryo ryagarutse gute ?
- Uwarizanye wese yakankoreye.
- Inumire ndagiye kuzana urwembe, ndahakebere hagishushe.
- Ariko ntumbabaze ! Iminsi yose nama ntsitara kuri iri no. Ewe wa no waragowe !
Yoo... ! Ehe aya maraso yose. Mpamagarira mama aze kundaba, ndapfuye.
- Ihorere ntaco uba, ngira nze kukuzanira umuti.
Ivyo vyose Nzeyimana yabivuga apfunagirika kandi ashishitira ino ryiwe.
