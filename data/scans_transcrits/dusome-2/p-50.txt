Abavukanyi
Iwacu tuvukana turi abana bane. Ni je ndi umuhererezi. Imfura ni Ndibanje, ubuheta ni Gacoreke, agakurikirwa na Harimenshi.
Abavyeyi bacu baradukunda na twe turabakunda.
Baratugaburiye ibikorwa dushobora kubafashamwo. Ndibanje bamushinze kuragira. Nta na rimwe yonesha. Ejobundi twagiye tubona data amuzaniye uduhuzu twiza two kumushimira. Gacoreke na we afasha mama mu bikorwa vy'i muhira. Na twe nta kindi badushinze atari ukwiga.
Ku mugoroba, twese duhwaniye i muhira, turayaga ; mbere hari naho tuvyina. Ariko ntakitunezereza nk'iyo abavyeyi batwiganiye ivya kera.
