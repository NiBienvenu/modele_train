Bamire yinjira inzu nshasha
Ku musi ugira gatatu, abasakara bazinduka umutwenzi, ubwatsi bwo bari baraburunze. Babanza gukindikisha inzu ubwatsi bugufibufi n'isakamburira kuko ari vyo biyifata. Isakamburira ribuza umuswa kurya ubwatsi n'imiganda. Mu kuyisakara, bamwe baruriye hejuru, abasigaye hasi na bo batekera imikama y'ubwatsi, baja barabaterera, gushika inzu yuzure. Imaze kwuzura, baciye bahereza abasakazi inkoko y'indya barirayo. Bavyita ubwururukanzu. Aho kuba ari uguhamura inzu ngo ntizove. Muri ico gihe, Bamire na we yaciye acana mu nzu, muvyarawe afata igishirira, ahagarara imbere y'umuryango, akirenza ya nzu, ati : «Ntaye imyotsi». Aho rero yabigira ngo inzu izogire imyotsi mike.
Bukeye bubaka umuryango, barawusakara, haba hasigaye gusikura. Imbere yo gusikura inzu, haje umuhinga azi kugera inkingi. Amaze kuzigera, baca barazitiba, baziha imikondo, bazambika imyashi, barazimbururira, barazishinga, imwe yose bayiha ikoma ryayo, hanyuma barazikakamura. Iyo nzu ya Bamire ifise inkingi cumi na zibiri. Hari iyo mu mbari, iyo mu bwicaro, zibiri zo mu mpete no mu rwuririzo rw'uburiri ; zibiri z'imisego, zitatu z'urusika n'imikingo, zindi zibiri z'agakingiraziko, n'iyindi yo mu muryango ifata agasego k'ikirambi. Izo nkingi zimaze gushingwa, baterako insika, imikingo, imisego, n'agakingiraziko. Vyose bimaze gutungana, bubaka uburiri, urusenge, uruhimbi n'ikirambi. Ivyo vyose biheze, Bamire abaterurira inzoga.
