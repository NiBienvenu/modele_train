Kuroba no kubaza vyamyeho mu Burundi
[igishushanyo: abana baroba ku ruzi]
Nyinabo wa Bayaga yabonye buratamanzuye, agenda amuherekeje kuko yaza kuzana isekuro babajishije kwa Bavakure. Bashitseyo, basanga abantu benshi baje gutora ivyo babajishije. Ari ubwato bwo kugana, bwo kwuhira, bwo kuroba, ari ivyansi, imihimbabweze, indosho canke amasekuro. Bayaga abonye Bavakure arungitse abaje gutwara ivyabo, amubaza ati : «Mbega ivyo vyose mbonye ubikura he[?] ?» Bavakure amwishura ati : «Ivyansi ndabibaza mu giti c'umwungo[?]. Ibindi na vyo nkabibaza mu mitinzi, mu misave no mu mivugangoma. Ariko ndondera canecane ibiti bitabungwa.» Bayaga arabaza[?] ... [?] imisi yose ... [?] ivyo bagutumye gusa[?] ?
