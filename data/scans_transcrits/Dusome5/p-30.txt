Ngengetereze ati : «Turabanza tukaja gutata ubutare, twabwubura tukabwimba, tukabuzana mu ruganda. Tuba twaraciye ibiti vy'iminazi, vy'imishwankima canke vy'iminyamabuye, tukabicamwo amakara». Agejeje aho, baba barashitse mu ruganda. Bashira amakara mu cokezo, bakira umuvuba baravuguta. Ya makara arungurije, bashiramwo ubutare. Bumaze gutukura, barabwokoza imbago, babutereka kw'ibuye ry'icuriro. Batora inyundo ari babiri. Umwe aturuka hino, uwundi hirya barabukubita. Babonye bumaze guhora babusubiza mu ziko, barongera baravuguta. Butukuye barabwokora, babusubiza muri rya buye. Barasubira barakubita na za nyundo. Bashishikara gutyo gushika aho babucamwo ibimanyu. Baca babicuramwo amasuka, imihoro, imarabiti, amacumu n'imbazo. Ivyo bagira vyose Bayaga ntiyabakurako ijisho. Ivyo biheze, Ngengetereze abwira Bayaga ngo amuhereze vya bigegene vy'umuhoro bazanye. Aca abigereka ku kuma kagaramye, yongerako n'ibindi bice vy'amafuni, vy'imihoro n'ivy'imbezi yari asanganywe, ashirako amakara, mugenziwe aravuguta, vya bimanyu birafatana. Bimaze gusa n'ikibengebenge, barengenga batereka ku mabuye y'icuriro, barahonda buhorobuhoro gushika bifatane, bacuramwo imihoro ine. Ngengetereze atora umwe awuhereza Bayaga ati : «Shira so, umushasha azoza kuwutora anzaniye amafaranga».
Bayaga arataha, ashitse i muhira ahereza wa muhoro se, aca amubwira ivyo wa mucuzi yamutumye.
Insiguro y'amajambo agoye
Gukoma ishwashwa : kuganiriza umuntu ari wenyene.
Uruganda : aho bacurira.
Umuvuba : ico bavugutisha mu kwatsa amakara iyo bariko baracura.
Imbago : icuma bokoza ibindi vyuma bitukuye iyo bariko baracura.
