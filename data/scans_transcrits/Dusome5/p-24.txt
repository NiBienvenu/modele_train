wapfuye, akawombokana no mu rutara kwa inabukwe ngo nwe ! Bugorovye Runyonga acuye, araba ya myagazi yiwe abona hasigaye umwe, uwundi arawubura. Baja mu vyambu rero. Mu gihe bakirondera, Inarunyonga akaguma yoherereza avuga ngo : «Muraronderera no mu rutara rwa Runyonga yemwe, agatakaye nta ho katarondererwa». Umukazana wiwe na we ati : «Ego yemwe muraronderera hose no mu rutara kwa mabukwe, ntabesha agatakaye nta ho katarondererwa». Erega barondeye kera, baja basanga umuvyimba w'uwo mwagazi, uri mu rutara kwa Inarunyonga. Ngo abibone atyo arajongoroka, atangura gutinya uwo mukazana wiwe, kuko yasanze banganya ubwenge. Ariko ntiyahaha. Uwundi musi, ahengera umukazana aroye gusenya, Inarunyonga aragenda aca imisigati, aza arayisigaturira mu nzira y'umukazana, kugira bamwirukane ngo yariye imisigati. Muka Runyonga avuye gusenya, asanga inabukwe akiyisigatura, aterera akaruru ku munwa, igihugu kirashwabaduka kuraba ikibaye. Abantu bamushikiriye, ati : «Nagira ngo murabe ivyo mabukwe yariko aragira». Inarunyonga arateterwa, arahaha, ntiyaba agisubira kubeshera umukazana.
Umugani uheze, agatiro kaba karabafashe, barabasasira bararyama.
Insiguro y'amajambo agoye
Kuja mu vyambu : kurondera hose.
Inzogera ya Runyonga rwanje ni yaca urwevu izovuga : uvuze ugishika hari akantu ni wamenyera.
Na yo ikaba ihigisha Runyonga rwawe : nanje nzoba namenyerejwe n'umuhungu wawe.
