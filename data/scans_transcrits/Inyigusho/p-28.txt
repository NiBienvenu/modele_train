Tumenye
Aho tuba hari ibidukikije vyinshi cane. Isi ndimwa, ibiti, amashamba,
ivyatsi, inzuzi, ibiyaga, ibikoko, inyoni, ifi, imisozi, amabuye n'ibindi.
Ibidukikije biradufitiye akamaro kanini :
- Isi : Turayubakako ; turayirimako imirima, ivamwo ubutare bukoreshwa
mu gukora ivyuma, n'ibindi.
- Ibiterwa : Bivamo imfungurwa, inkwi, imbaho, intebe,bikoreshwa mu
kubaka amazu, birakingira isi inkukura, birazana akayaga
keza, birakwega imvura,n'ibindi...
- Ibikoko : Biratanga imfungurwa ( inyama, amata, amavuta).Biratanga
inshato ubwoya bishobora gukoreshwa mu gukora ivyambarwa (impuzu n'ibirato). Biratanga ifumbire n'ibindi...
Dukingire rero ibidukikije mu kwirinda guturira amashamba no kuyonona
mu bundi buryo.
Igikorwa ca 15: Ibikorwa rusangi
Kana na Barumwete ni abana bato. Ariko barihweje akazi
gakorerwa mu bikorwa rusangi barabishima. Umunsi umwe niho baja
kubishengerera baca batangura kuganira bati :
Kana : Ehe bariya bantu Baru ! Ngira ni ibikorwa rusangi.
