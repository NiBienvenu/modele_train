Igikorwa ca 15 : Ikiyago ca Bukuru na Nahimana
Umusi umwe Bukuru na Nahimana bari bavuye kw'ishure bagenda baraganira ivyerekeye igikorwa co gukorera i muhira umwigisha yari yahaye
abanyeshure bo mu mwaka wa kane Bukuru yigamwo.
Bukuru : Igikorwa kiraho mwigisha yaduhaye kiragoye ! Ngo tuvuge ibikorwa bijanye n'ibihe? Mbega ibihe vyovyo yashatse kuvuga
iki? Jewe nzohava ndonka ubusa.
Nahimana : Oya nawe nta busa woronka! Natwe ico gikorwa barakiduhaye
mwakuyu, naciye ngenda kubaza abavyeyi barakimfasha.
Ibihe vyovyo n'ubu ndavyibuka. Hariho agatasi, umukubezi,
urushana, impeshi n'ici. Navyo ibikorwa barangura muri ivyo
bihe, nkeka bihindagurika ku ntara.
Bukuru : Ndavyumvise nzogerageza. Ndazi ko mu ci bahejeje kwimbura imirima yo ku misozi (ibiharage, amasaka, ubushaza,...), baca batangura kurima mu myonga. Baharimira
ibiharage n'ibigori bizokwimburwa ku mutasuro. Ico gihe baca
batera ibiharage n'ibigori vyo ku musozi. Ivyo navyo bavyimbura mu rushana.
