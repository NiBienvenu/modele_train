Igikorwa ca 13 : Ndagupfinze !
Nkundwa, Ntwari na Kazubwenge bariko barapfindana. Umvirize :
Nkundwa : Kazubwenge, ndagufinze, aho hambere ku munsi
w'ubukwe vyagenda gute ?
Kazubwenge : Ababanyi, incuti, abagenzi barashaza bakaja gushigikira
nyene urubanza baberewe. Bagenda bashoreye imibindi
y' inzoga n'ibikemanyi vyuzuye bamuterereye, nawe akabaterekera neza, bagasangira urukanywa, imfu-ngurwa
na canecane ijambo ry'urubanza. Mu guserura akanyamuneza bafise ku mutima, baravyina, bagatamba bagatarika, n'ivyatsi bikanoga.
Ntwari : Nanje ndagufinde :
Mu mibano yabo ya minsi yose ho vyagenda gute ?
Karubwenge : Bagira bati umubanyi niwe muryango, bakagenderanira,
bagafashanya muri vyose, no mu ndero y'urwaruka,
umwana yari uwa bose, yakeburwa n'umuvyeyi wese
amubonye mw'ikosa.
Nkundwa : Weho ga Ntwari, amatati yadutse mu kibano, ba Sokuru
bagira iki ?
Ntwari : Barumviriza abatase, uwuri mu kuri bakamushigikira,
uwutagufise bakamuhanura, yanse bakamutorera igihano. Ivyo vyose babigira bashize imbere ukuri, badasunitswe n'umutima w'amaronko.
Karubwenge : Nkundwa, natwe tugusubirize mu ndumane. Mbega bagwanya inzara gute ?
Nkundwa : Umwe wese yaritwararika imirimo ajejwe, abarima bakarima
bakirinda gusesagura no gusahura, umwimbu ukagwira
muri vyose, bikaba magiriranire, gutyo umwe wese akaronka ibimukwiye, zaba imfungurwa canke ibindi akeneye
mu buzima.
Ntwari : Tuve kuri basokuru tuvuge abariho tubona ubu.Nkundwa,
abavyeyi n'abarezi bategura kazoza k'urwaruka n'igihugu
gute ?
