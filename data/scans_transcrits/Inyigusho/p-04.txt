GUSHIKIRIZA
Reta y'Uburundi iramaze imyaka ibiri y'ishure itanguje inyigisho y'indero
runtu no gukundisha igihugu mu mashure y'intango, ni ukuvuga kuva mu
mwaka w'ishure 2006 ushira 2007. Ubu turi mu mwaka ugira gatatu iyo
nyigisho yigishwa mu ndero y'urwaruka rw'i Burundi ruri mu mashure.
Nkuko ukurera ari uguhozako, Reta yateye iyindi ntambwe mu ntumbero
yihaye yo kubiba hakiri kare imbuto nziza z'indero runtu no gukundisha igihugu c'amavukiro mu rwaruka, gurtyo ikazitira itaronerwa.
Twibukanye ko iyo nyigisho yatanguriye mu myaka ya mbere n'iya kabiri
hanyuma irashika mu myaka ya gatatu mu mwaka w'ishure 2007 ushira
2008. Aka gatabu kaje gutsimbataza kongera kungura ubumenyi ku
banyeshure bo mu myaka ya gatatu n'iya kane kuko abari mu myaka ya
kabiri n'iya gatatu uno munsi, uyu mwaka w'ishure wa 2009 ushira 2010
bazoba bashitse mu myaka ya gatatu n'iya kane.
Banyeshure, kerebuke mukorane n'abarezi babafashe kumenya no
kugendera inyifato zibereye mu buzima bwanyu bwa minsi yose.
Mu kubafasha, abarezi babasaba guserura ivyiyumviro vyanyu mubihanahane hagati yanyu no hagati yanyu n'abarezi, gutyo mwunganire bimwe
bishemeye indero yanyu ni naho inyigisho muzokuramwo zobanyura
mukazishira mu ngiro mukabera n'abandi akarorero keza mu nyifato nziza
zubaka.
Umushikiranganji w'Inyigisho
y'amashure y'intango n'ayisumbuye
Ernest MBERAMIHETO
