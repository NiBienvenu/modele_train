ISHINGIRO RYA MBERE : UKUMENYA UKO URI N'UKO ABANDI BARI
Igikorwa ca 1 : Sinoheba i wacu !
Mbega ko wama wambaye agahuzu kamwe ?
Ntaco bintwaye kazima kamesuye kandi kakaba kankwiye
I muhira kwa ba Congera barakenye cane. Uwo mwana yama yambaye
impuzu imwe kw'ishure. Umunsi umwe umwana bigana Gacoreke yamubwiye ati : "Urabona ingene wama mu gahuzu kamwe ? Ingo tugende i
wacu uze kwiberayo bazokugurira izindi mpuzu."
Congera : "Oya ni ukuri sinshobora guheba abavyeyi ngo barakenye ."
Gacoreke : "Uri ikijuju basi ! Uraronka ivyagusa ntuvyemere?"
Congera : "Pu ! Ja hiyo sha, sinshobora kuja gushumba mfise i wacu,
kubera ubukene."
Utubazo
a. Mubona iki kuri iyo shusho ?
b. Muhira kwa ba Congera bari bamerewe gute ?
c. Uwo Gacoreke yabwiye ngw'iki Congera ?
d. Ivyo yamubwiye yavyakiriye gute ?
e. Muri abo bana babiri, mushigikiye nde ?
f. Mwohanura iki abandi bana bameze nk'uwo mugenzi wa Congera ?
Tumenye
Ni vyiza kumenya ko twese tutameze kumwe canke tutamerewe kumwe.
Hariho abatunze, abakenye, abakomeye, abamugaye, abazi ubwenge
mw'ishure, ibijuju mw'ishure, n'abandi. Ariko rero ivyo vyose ntivyotubera
intambamyi ngo bitume twikumira canke ngo dukengere abandi mu
mibano yacu ya minsi yose.
