Bahejeje gufungura mw'ijoro, aho bokwicaye ngo bace imigani, nyina arabahinda ngo baje kuryama. Bashitse ku kirambi, Bisanga akura iminoni
ayiha Rweza ngo nawe ayirarane, apfume ashaza mw'itiro ko banse ko
bayigabura ku mutaga. Ba bigeme barasinzira.
Harya mu gicugu hagati, wa mugore arasoboka, buhorobuhoro, arakorakora ba bana ngo yumve uwutambaye iminoni. Aca amusogota ya nkota
mu mutima, aramwica. Aca asubira inyuma aho yari aryamye.
Mu mutwenzi wa kare arazinduka ngo aje guta ca kiziga. Ngo agire ngo
arafukura, emwe ! Ukwiyahura kuraguma ; asanga yaraye yihekuye.
Aririra mu nda ariko aca arondera ubwenge bwo kunyegeza ico kiziga
Rweza atarikangura. Agiterurira hanze areraguza aho yogishira arahebura. Inyoni zivuze, afata ca kiziga, agishira mu gisamba yahora ayoresha
umwavu arikorera aragenda. Ashitse mu kinogo yatamwo umwavu, yumva
ijwi rya wa mwana rihakana ngo, um,um,um .
Wa mugore agerageza gushira ca gisamba hasi, wajahe !
Agerageza ngo ibirimwo biseseke, mpera umbone !
Aragenda mw'ishamba ku ruzi, arurira imisozi, aramanuka mu tubande,
arajabuka imfunzo, biranka ko amuta aho amubajije hose. Haheze imisi
itatu agenda , aserukira ku rugo ruri munsi y'umusozi, abaza Bisanga ati:
"Ndaguhambe aha ?" Rya jwi riremera "eeeeee". Wa mugore aratura
ningoga na ningoga ca gisamba, agitereka hasi. Yunamuye amaso abona
Rweza na Se bavuye mu nzu. Abakubise amaso yikubita hasi iruhande ya
ca gisamba aca aracikana yishwe n'uburuhe, agahinda canke ishari ntawubizi.
Rweza na se bafuba Bisanga na nyina. Si je nohahera hohera ishari.
