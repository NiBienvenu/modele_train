Utubazo
a. Muri ico gisomwa bavugamwo bande ?
b. Abo bavugwa bariko baganira ivyerekeye iki ?
c. Ico gikorwa cari cerekeye iki ?
d. None ibihe vyo mu mwaka ni ibihe ?
e. Vuga ibikorwa abantu bakora mu gihe kimwekimwe.
Tumenye
Iyo umwaka ugenze neza, habamwo ibihe bikurikira : agatasi, umukubezi,
urushana, impeshi, ici.
Mu bihe vy'imvura (agatasi n'urushana) abarimyi baca batera ku musozi
ibiterwa bitandukanye ( ibiharage, ibigori, ubushaza,...), bikamera, bigakura, bakabibagara hanyuma bikera.
Mu bihe vy'izuba ( ku mukubezi no mu ci ), ibiterwa vyo mu gatasi n'ivyo
mu rushana biba vyeze. Baca babisarura,
bagaca barimira igihe gikwirikira.
Mu myonga baharima mu ci, bakahatera ibiterwa bitandukanye. Bimwe
baraheza bakabivomera (amashu, ibitunguru, amatomate,...), ibindi navyo
usanga bidasaba amazi menshi cane ntibarinde kubivomera (ibijumbu, ibiharage, ibigori).
