Igikorwa ca 12 : Akuzuye ntikamurikwa !
Mu karuhuko ka Noweli Gakunzi yagiye kuramutsa sekuru, umutama
akuze. Ashitse mu nzira, imvura iragwa, Gakunzi yugama ku muhana yari
ashitseko. Imvura ihise, arabandanya inzira yiwe. Naho bwariko burira,
nta bwoba yarafise kuko yaja arahura n'abantu mu nzira, bakamuramutsa,
bakamuyagisha, akumva araremye.
Ashitse kwa Sekuru, yaranezerewe cane kuko yasanze Sekuru
abayeho neza.
Ababanyi baramufasha muri vyose, baramwegeraniriza ibintu ntanumwe
amugirira nabi.
Ivuriro riri hafi yiwe rirakora neza, arashobora kwivuza abikeneye. Si
ivuriro gusa rimwegereye, hariho n'abarimyi n'aborozi ba kijambere,
ashatse ikintu kitari mw'itongo iwe n'amata ntibimugora kuronka. Aba
kandi hafi y'ibarabara, imiduga itwara ingenzi, n'iyishora ibidandazwa
iguma inyuragiranwa, ari n'ico atubwira birara bidushikiriye.
Utwana tuja kw'ishure natwo, duhora duhita turamwuzukuza, ngo Sogoku,
erega turagusize kw'ishure, mwigisha araguhana niwacererwa.
Nawe agatwenga ati : "Murahimbaye mwa bana. Ba muragenda ndabahambije."
Eka akuzuye ntikamurikwa, Sogokuru ntamaganya arorohewe.
Utubazo
a. Mu karuhuko ka Noweli Gakunzi yagiye hehe ?
b. Mu nzira vyamugendeye gute ?
c. Ashitse kwa sekuru yanezerewe n'iki ?
d. Ni ibiki vyerekana ko Sekuru abayeho neza ?
Tumenye
Amahoro azana ivyiza vyinshi.Abantu barakundana bagafashanya mu
buzima bwabo bwa minsi yose.
Nta wama afise umutima uhagaze kuko atawugirira nabi uwundi, aho uri
hose, wumva uremye. Abantu barakora ata nkomanzi, iterambere rikarama.
